## What the shape says

- **Combinations (1,483)** are the largest identity category but by definition cannot
  change a single-compound headline count: labelled and retained (`is_combination`).
- **Description-only (1,791)** is the largest overall: the compound appears only in an
  intervention description. Under §5.1a this can never be accepted; most is background
  therapy or co-medication. Triage: only the 770 suggested-experimental, systemic rows matter.
- **Parenthetical (144)**: "Compound (BRAND, Manufacturer, Location)" and similar — 88
  headline-relevant. Rule-addressable, but a rule here **adds** auto-accepts, so under the
  measurement policy it would trigger re-measurement. One caveat seen: "75 mg acetylsalicylic
  acid (ASA) + 1250 mg calcium carbonate" — "calcium carbonate" is stripped as salt words, so
  the parenthetical-plus-combination case needs care.
- **Device context in "qualified_other"**: stent, eluting, balloon, scaffold, catheter,
  Cypher, bioresorbable (≈ 400 word hits) — drug-eluting devices, local route, cannot move
  a count.
- **Defects found (all downgrade-only to fix):**
  - "X placebo" names ("ASA placebo", "Acarbose Placebo") are placebos *for* the compound
    and should be rejected; the placebo rule only recognises "placebo of X" / "X-matching
    placebo".
  - "plus" is not a combination separator ("aspirin plus clopidogrel" lands in
    qualified_other, not combination).
  - "lithium disilicate" (dental ceramic, 33 hits) should be a lithium must_not_match.
- **Small, genuinely human categories**: class-level (58), short codes (36), near-misses
  (60), fixture review lists including the polyamine review queries (312).

## Triage (standing)

Review only items that can change a headline count: suggested role `experimental`,
`systemic` exposure, not a combination, for a compound on an output path. Everything else
stays labelled in the queue and is not reviewed. On this basis the queue to review is
1,892 rows, before any rule-based reduction. Role suggestions
are themselves only 78.6% accurate (step 4), so this triage inherits that error until role
is measured (`docs/role1_protocol.md`).

## Known limitation, noted and deliberately not fixed now

The other-drug rule (`other_drug_stems` in `data/rules/resolver.yaml`, added in v2 for the
step-4 regimen error) recognises "another drug" in `otherNames` only as another fixture
compound or an entry carrying one of a short list of INN stems. Any other drug (an
antibiotic, a statin, a brand with no stem) is not recognised, so a single-compound name
whose `otherNames` lists it is not sent to review. Doing this properly needs a drug-name
dictionary (e.g. ChEMBL or RxNorm names), not a longer stem list. Decided 2026-11-18: note
it, do not fix it now. The miss is in the accepting direction, so it is within what the
step-4b precision measurement (99.5%, a floor under resolver-v3) already counts.
